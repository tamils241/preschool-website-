Eligibility

Age eligibility

Our programs welcome children from 1.5 to 6 years. Placement is based on age and readiness, and may vary according to school policy.

  • Toddler: 1.5 – 2.5 years
  • Playgroup: 2 – 3 years
  • Nursery: 3 – 4 years
  • Lower Kindergarten: 4 – 5 years
  • Upper Kindergarten: 5 – 6 years
Checklist

Required documents

Please keep these ready when you complete your enrolment. Scans or copies are fine.

  • Child's birth certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Parent / guardian ID proof
  • Up-to-date immunisation record
  • Address proof

Document requirements are indicative and may be updated by school policy.
