Welcome to Stackly

Where every child feels at home and ready to explore

Stackly Preschool is a child-centred early learning centre for children aged 1.5 to 6 years. We blend structured learning with free play, so children build language, motor, social and thinking skills at their own happy pace.

  • Play-based curriculum designed by early-childhood experts
  • Small class groups with individual attention
  • Safe, hygienic and CCTV-monitored campus
  • Daily progress updates for parents
