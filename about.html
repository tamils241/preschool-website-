Who We Are

A joyful home for early learning

What began as a small neighbourhood playgroup has grown into a thriving early-learning centre trusted by hundreds of local families. Throughout our journey, one thing has never changed: our commitment to knowing every child as an individual.

We believe the early years shape a lifetime of learning. That is why every Stackly classroom is designed for wonder — filled with stories, music, colour, movement and endless opportunities to explore.

Educational Philosophy

A child-centred learning approach

We follow a play-based, child-led philosophy inspired by early-childhood best practice. Teachers observe each child's interests and gently build learning experiences around them, so curiosity always leads the way.

  • Learning through play, exploration and imagination
  • Small groups for genuine individual attention
  • Social and emotional growth alongside academics
  • Regular family communication and partnership
